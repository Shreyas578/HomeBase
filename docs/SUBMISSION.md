# Qloo LLM Hackathon Submission Summary

### Project Name: HomeBase 🏛️
**Elevator Pitch (4 Lines):**
HomeBase is an autonomous taste-intelligence agent that eliminates the isolation of relocating international students by mapping their personal cultural frequency into unfamiliar university cities. Powered by Qloo's taste graph, OpenStreetMap, and Groq LLMs, it discovers verified taste-twin third places with exact walking times (SETTLE), computes cross-domain discovery bridges to match kindred peers (CONNECT), and explains hard academic topics through student-beloved media with rigorous boundary breakdown limits (LEARN).
